Non-Destructive Testing & Structural Health Monitoring Group

Welcome to the NDT & SHM Group led by Dr. Liangliang Cheng at the University of Groningen. We develop advanced solutions for non-destructive testing and structural health monitoring.

Our research combines dynamics and vibration, smart sensing and advanced measurements, experimental mechanics, signal processing, and physics-informed data-driven modelling to understand, monitor, and predict the behaviour of engineering structures.

Meet the Group Recent Publications

About

Dynamics, sensing, and data-driven modelling for safer structures.

We work across a wide range of applications, including mechanical systems, civil infrastructure, aerospace and composite structures, railway systems, additive manufacturing, and renewable energy systems. By bridging fundamental dynamics with practical engineering challenges, our group aims to support safer, more reliable, and more sustainable structures.

We welcome motivated PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, visiting scholars, master and bachelor students who are interested in vibration, sensing, non-destructive evaluation, structural health monitoring, nonlinear dynamics, and data-driven modelling.

Research

Research themes and recent highlights

Additive Manufacturing

Vibration-based NDT for metal additive manufacturing

AMVibe develops vibration-based inspection methods for quality assessment of metal additive manufactured components.

Computer Vision

Camera-based dynamic vibration analysis

Camera tracking, motion analysis, and Dynamic Mode Decomposition support non-contact full-field vibration measurements.

Physics-Informed AI

Physics-informed neural networks for structural vibrations

VibPINNs connect sparse measurements with forward and inverse problems in structural vibration prediction.

Infrastructure

Monitoring bridges and railway systems

The group studies dynamic monitoring, track irregularity estimation, and distributed fibre-optic sensing for infrastructure.

Opportunities for students and researchers

We are always interested in working with motivated students and researchers who are passionate about dynamics, vibration, non-destructive testing, structural health monitoring, smart sensing, and data-driven modelling.

The NDT & SHM Group welcomes applications from prospective PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, visiting PhD students, visiting scholars, master thesis students, and research interns.
