October 2025

Industry lecture on sensing and monitoring by Olivier De Moor from KonneXXions BV

The group was delighted to host Olivier De Moor from KonneXXions BV at Engineering and Technology Institute Groningen for a training and demonstration of Polytec's VibroFlex QTec system. Olivier delivered a superb presentation and showcased mechanical, optical, and biomedical use cases.

Special thanks go to Prof. Anastasiia Krushynska for the lab space and for making it an interactive experience with students, and to Olga Guseva for her kind coordination.

The group looks forward to future collaborations.
