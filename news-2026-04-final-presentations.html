Final presentations on CNC tool wear and stability lobe analysis

Today, Matthijs Neerhof and Dayon Benning delivered the final presentation of their research projects on:

  1. 1. Tool Wear Classification Using Pretrained Vision Models and Multi-Signal Fusion
  2. 2. Stability Lobe Analysis of Lathing Machines in CNC Manufacturing Lines

Both projects addressed highly relevant challenges in advanced manufacturing, with a strong focus on improving process reliability, product quality, and intelligent monitoring in CNC production environments.

The first project explored how pretrained vision models, combined with multi-signal data fusion, can support more robust and automated tool wear classification. The second project focused on stability lobe analysis for lathing machines, contributing to a better understanding of chatter stability and machining performance in industrial CNC lines.

We am very pleased with their presentations and the work they have carried out. Both students showed strong commitment, technical depth, and independence throughout their projects. It was great to see how they developed their ideas and translated them into meaningful research outcomes.

During their research project journey, I would also like to sincerely thank Ger Folkersma, Remco Poelarends and Johan de Wit from Demcon high-tech systems for the great support and guidance provided throughout the process, and second supervisor Nabeel Younas, PMP®, as well as my PhD students Hailong Liu and Wenbo Zhang！

Congratulations again to both of them on this achievement, and I wish them all the best in their future careers.
