Opportunities for students and researchers

We are always interested in working with motivated students and researchers who are passionate about dynamics, vibration, non-destructive testing, structural health monitoring, smart sensing, and data-driven modelling.

The NDT & SHM Group welcomes applications from prospective PhD candidates, postdoctoral researchers, visiting PhD students, visiting scholars, master thesis students, and research interns. We are particularly interested in candidates with backgrounds in mechanical engineering, civil engineering, aerospace engineering, electrical engineering, applied mathematics, computer science, robotics, or related fields.
