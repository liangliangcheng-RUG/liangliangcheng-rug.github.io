Infrared Thermography for Non-destructive Testing

Virtual Wave Concept

Heat diffusion spreads and smooths thermal information. Virtual-wave reconstruction transforms measured temperature responses into a wave-like field, making depth-dependent features easier to localize.

Heat diffusion

(∇2−1α∂∂t) T(r,t)= −1αT0(r)δ(t)

Diffusive spreading; irreversible in forward time.

Virtual wave propagation

(∇2−1c2∂2∂t2) p(r,t)= −1c2∂∂t [p0(r)δ(t)]

Directional propagation; reversible in the ideal lossless model.

Here, T is the temperature field, p is the reconstructed virtual-wave field, α is thermal diffusivity, and c is the chosen virtual-wave speed. T0 and p0 denote initial fields, and δ(t) is the Dirac impulse.

The virtual wave is a mathematical reconstruction, rather than a directly measured acoustic wave. Recovering it from thermal data is an inverse problem that requires regularization.
