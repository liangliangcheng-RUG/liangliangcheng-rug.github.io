Research

Research themes and recent highlights

Additive Manufacturing

Vibration-based NDT for metal additive manufacturing

AMVibe develops vibration-based inspection methods for quality assessment of metal additive manufactured components.

Computer Vision

Camera-based dynamic vibration analysis

Camera tracking, motion analysis, and Dynamic Mode Decomposition support non-contact full-field vibration measurements.

Physics-Informed AI

Physics-informed neural networks for structural vibrations

VibPINNs connect sparse measurements with forward and inverse problems in structural vibration prediction.

Infrastructure

Monitoring bridges and railway systems

The group studies dynamic monitoring, track irregularity estimation, and distributed fibre-optic sensing for infrastructure.
