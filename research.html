Infrared Thermography for Non-destructive Testing

Virtual Wave Concept

Developing thermal-wave imaging, virtual-wave reconstruction, and advanced thermographic signal processing for defect detection and material characterization.

Virtual-wave reconstruction maps diffusive temperature responses into a wave-like field for depth-resolved defect localization.

Heat diffusion and virtual-wave equations

Heat diffusion

(∇2−1α∂∂t) T(r,t)= −1αT0(r)δ(t)

Diffusive spreading; irreversible in forward time.

Virtual wave propagation

(∇2−1c2∂2∂t2) p(r,t)= −1c2∂∂t [p0(r)δ(t)]

Directional propagation; reversible in the ideal lossless model.

T: temperature field; p: reconstructed virtual-wave field; α: thermal diffusivity; c: chosen virtual-wave speed. T0 and p0 are the initial fields, and δ(t) is the Dirac impulse. Reconstructing the virtual wave from thermal data requires regularization.
