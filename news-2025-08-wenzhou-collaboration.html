August 2025

Collaboration visit with Wenzhou University and Wenzhou Medical University

I was honored to be invited by Professor Dehua Zhu from Wenzhou University 温州大学 University and Dr. Yushan Yu from Wenzhou Medical University 温州医科大学 Medical University, in China, to explore potential collaborations in innovative AI applications for smart manufacturing and biomedical engineering.

Over several productive days, we developed valuable ideas for our next steps and also discussed establishing student exchange and internship programs between Engineering and Technology institute Groningen and them.

I am excited about the future possibilities and look forward to advancing our collaboration soon!
